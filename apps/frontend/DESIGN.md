# Precedent: UI notes

## Product

Help small builders, community groups, planners and housing teams find and understand how similar housing applications fared in planning. The interface is an evidence browser, not an outcome predictor.

## Main flow

1. Describe a proposed housing development and choose a council.
2. Read the headline: the share of decided similar applications that were granted, and the typical time to a decision.
3. Browse the closest matching cases, in the list and on the map, and open any case's council record.
4. Select a result to move the map and keep the list and map in sync. Tap the map to compare your site with nearby areas.

## Visual direction

A builder's site-notice look: ink and hi-vis yellow, hard offset shadows, Archivo condensed lettering for headings, Inter for body text. Keep the evidence easy to scan and the map secondary to the records.

Decision status uses consistent colours: granted (green), refused (red), withdrawn or invalid (grey). An appeal is a purple ring around the decision colour, not a fourth decision. Status is always labelled in text as well as colour.

## Trust and data

All figures come from the national planning register through the planning API. Show the number of similar applications next to every figure and keep caveats next to the claims they qualify. Word delays as comparisons, never causes, and never say how a new application "will" go.
