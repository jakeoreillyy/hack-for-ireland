# Planning Precedent Explorer — UI notes

## Product

Help residents, community groups, planners, and housing teams make past planning decisions easier to find and understand. The interface is an evidence browser, not an outcome predictor.

## Main flow

1. Describe a proposed housing development and choose a planning authority.
2. Review the proposal fields extracted for comparison.
3. Browse comparable applications and inspect the cases behind each explanation.
4. Select a result to move the map and keep the list, explanation, and map in sync.

## Visual direction

Use a calm, civic editorial style: warm off-white surfaces, forest-green actions, restrained borders, Newsreader display type, and Inter for controls and body text. Keep the evidence easy to scan and the map secondary to the records.

Decision status uses consistent colors: granted (green), refused (red), appealed (amber), and pending (blue). Status is always labeled in text as well as color.

## Trust and data

The current records and coordinates are synthetic examples. Label them as illustrative in the interface. Do not describe them as live, official, or sourced from a register. The AI extraction and case explanation are previews until an actual AI service is connected. Keep caveats next to the claims they qualify.
